## Cach Xu Ly

### User khong biet tao video

Huong dan:

```text
/makevideo chu de video
```

Neu can video tot hon, goi y OPC:

```text
topic | style | duration | audience | goal | tone | quality
```

### User thieu credit

Gui `/pricing` va huong dan `/buy <goi>`.

### User bao video loi

Can xin:

- Thoi gian tao video.
- Chu de da gui.
- Anh man hinh loi.
- Neu co, job id hoac output path.

### User chuyen khoan

Yeu cau gui bill ro so tien, thoi gian, tai khoan nhan.
