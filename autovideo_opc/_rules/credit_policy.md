# Credit Policy

## Nguyen Tac Cot Loi

- Kiem tra credit truoc khi tao video.
- Khong tru credit neu video that bai.
- Chi tru credit sau khi video gui thanh cong.
- Admin co the cong/set credit nhung phai co quyen.

## Chi Phi

- quick: 2 credits.
- standard/fact: 4 credits.
- story: 4 credits.
- deep: 7 credits.
- premium: 10 credits.
