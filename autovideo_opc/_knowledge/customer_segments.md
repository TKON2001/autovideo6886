# Customer Segments

## Creator Ca Nhan

Nhu cau:

- Tao video ngan deu dan.
- Can y tuong, script, hinh anh va voice nhanh.
- Muon chi phi ro rang.

Can uu tien:

- Prompt de dung.
- Video nhanh va standard.
- Huong dan viet chu de tot.

## Seller Va Chu Shop

Nhu cau:

- Tao video ban hang, giai thich san pham, cau chuyen thuong hieu.
- Can caption ro, thong diep ngan.
- Muon video co the dang len social.

Can uu tien:

- Style marketing.
- Hook manh.
- CTA mem.

## Team Content

Nhu cau:

- Can credit lon.

Can uu tien:
