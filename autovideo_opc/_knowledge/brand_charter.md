# Brand Charter

## Dinh Vi

AutoVideo Bot la bot tao video AI tren Telegram cho creator, seller, team content va nguoi can san xuat video ngan nhanh. Bot tap trung vao video doc 9:16, de dang dung tren TikTok, Reels va YouTube Shorts.

## Loi Hua

User chi can nhap mot y tuong ro rang. Bot ho tro bien y tuong do thanh video hoan chinh gom script, hinh minh hoa, voice AI, caption va file MP4.

## Tinh Cach Thuong Hieu

- Nhanh gon nhung khong so sai.
- Thuc te, ro rang, khong noi qua.
- Huong den ket qua video co the dung duoc.
- Uu tien trai nghiem mobile.

## Dieu Khong Duoc Lam

- Khong tao cam giac bot la phep mau tao video hoan hao moi lan.
- Khong chen watermark, logo goc, badge canh vao video.
- Khong hua chinh xac thoi gian render neu chi co ETA uoc tinh.
- Khong lam video sai kich thuoc doc 9:16.

## Tieu Chuan Giao Tiep

Tin nhan bot can ngan, lich su, de hieu:

- Noi ro chi phi credit truoc khi tao.
- Noi ro loi va credit co bi tru hay khong.
- Huong dan user viet prompt tot hon.
- Khi thanh toan, noi ro chuyen khoan va gui bill cho admin.
