# Video Quality Standard

## Dinh Dang Bat Buoc

- Ty le: doc 9:16.
- Resolution: 1080x1920.
- FPS: 30.
- Format: MP4.
- Codec: H.264.
- Audio: AAC.

## Chat Luong Hinh Anh

Moi canh nen co hinh minh hoa rieng:

- Bo cuc doc phu hop mobile.
- Chu the ro.
- Anh sang cinematic hoac realistic.
- Nen co chieu sau.
- Mau sac co cam xuc.
- Khong text trong anh.
- Khong watermark, logo, UI, caption trong anh.

## Caption Va Mobile Safe Zone

Caption phai:

- Nam trong lower third nhung khong sat day.
- Cach mep trai/phai toi thieu 90px.
- Cach day toi thieu 240px, uu tien 330px tro len khi xem Telegram.
- De doc tren man hinh dien thoai.
- Nen co nen den ban trong suot hoac shadow.

Khong duoc:

- Dat text sat mep tren hoac mep duoi.
- Dat caption che mat chu the quan trong.
- Chen logo goc trai.
- Chen label "Canh 1" hoac badge goc phai.

## Audio

- Voice ro, khong qua nho.
- Nhac nen neu co chi nen 8-12% volume.
- Neu nhac nen lam kho nghe voice thi tat nhac.

## QC Dat Yeu Cau

Video dat yeu cau khi:

- File ton tai va > 0 byte.
- Mo duoc.
- Dung 1080x1920.
- Caption khong bi cat.
- Khong logo, watermark, badge canh.
- Credit chi bi tru sau khi video gui thanh cong.
