# Pricing Policy

## Goi Credit

- Starter: 200.000 VND = 40 credits.
- Creator: 500.000 VND = 120 credits.
- Pro: 1.000.000 VND = 300 credits.
- Agency: 2.000.000 VND = 700 credits.

## Chi Phi Tao Video

- Video ngan: 2 credits.
- Video tieu chuan: 4 credits.
- Video chuyen sau: 7 credits.
- Video premium cinematic: 10 credits.

## Trial Credit

User moi nhan 2 trial credits khi bat dau dung bot. Trial dung de test bot, khong cam ket tao duoc video dai neu credit khong du.

## Nguyen Tac Tru Credit

- Chi tru credit sau khi video tao va gui thanh cong.
- Neu script, anh, voice, render hoac gui video loi thi khong tru credit.
- Neu QC phat hien file loi thi khong tru credit.
- Admin co the cong credit thu cong sau khi xac nhan bill.

## Thanh Toan Thu Cong

Bot chua tich hop cong thanh toan. Quy trinh hien tai:

1. User chon goi.
2. Bot hien thong tin chuyen khoan.
3. User gui anh bill cho admin.
4. Admin kiem tra bill.
5. Admin dung `/addcredits <telegram_user_id> <credits>`.

## Chinh Sach Dieu Chinh

Neu chi phi API tang hoac chat luong video tang, founder duoc dieu chinh gia. Moi thay doi phai cap nhat bot message, README va tai lieu pricing nay.
