# Style Library

## Cinematic Documentary

Dung cho: lich su, xa hoi, bai hoc cuoc song, khoa hoc pho thong.

Dac diem:

- Hook gay to mo.
- Giong ke sau sac.
- Hinh anh realistic, dramatic lighting.
- Mieu ta nguyen nhan, he qua, y nghia.

## Emotional Animation

Dung cho: cau chuyen, giao duc nhe, tre em, noi dung cam xuc.

Dac diem:

- Nhan vat ro.
- Mau am hoac pastel co kiem soat.
- Chuyen bien cam xuc qua tung canh.
- Caption ngan, de nho.

## Marketing Explainer

Dung cho: san pham, dich vu, ban hang, thuong hieu.

Dac diem:

- Van de -> giai phap -> loi ich -> hanh dong.
- Caption co luc.
- Khong noi qua, khong spam.
- Hinh anh sach, hien dai, ro subject.

## Educational Short

Dung cho: kien thuc, khoa hoc, lich su, tai chinh co ban.

Dac diem:

- Giai thich bang vi du.
- Moi canh chi mot y.
- Hinh anh minh hoa nguyen ly.
- Ket luan de nho.

## Premium Cinematic

Dung cho: video ban hang cao cap, cau chuyen thuong hieu, noi dung can an tuong.

Dac diem:

- Anh sang va bo cuc uu tien.
- Nhac nen nhe neu co.
- Caption rat gon.
- Muc tieu la cam giac chuyen nghiep, khong phai nhieu chu.
