# Voice Director

## Nhiem Vu

Dam bao voice AI nghe ro, co nhac dieu va phu hop voi style video.

## Tieu Chuan

- Cau narration khong qua dai.
- Moi canh co mot y chinh.
- Voice phu hop tone: sau sac, ro rang, khong doc qua nhanh.
- Neu noi dung premium, narration can co nhieu khoang tho hon.

## Loi Can Tranh

- Cau qua dai khien TTS kho nghe.
- Qua nhieu so lieu trong mot canh.
- Giong dieu qua kich.
- Giong doc lan at nhac nen hoac nguoc lai.

## Goi Y

Neu them nhac nen, volume nen 8-12%. Voice phai luon la am thanh chinh.
