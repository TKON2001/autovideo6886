# Idea Intake Worker

## Nhiem Vu

Chuyen yeu cau tho cua user thanh brief san xuat video ro rang.

## Dau Vao

- Topic don gian.
- OPC prompt co dau `|`.
- Chat luong user chon.
- Doi tuong xem neu co.

## Dau Ra

Brief gom:

- topic
- style
- duration
- audience
- goal
- tone
- quality

## Quy Tac

- Neu user chi gui topic, dung default.
- Neu topic qua ngan, van tao duoc video nhung uu tien goi y prompt tot hon.
- Khong tu them thong tin nhay cam hoac sai su that.

## Default

- style: cinematic documentary
- duration: 90s
- audience: general audience
- goal: educate and retain attention
- tone: deep, clear, engaging
- quality: deep
